## Trade Autopsy Checklist

For each trade:

- setup type named
- entry, stop, target, exit recorded
- R result calculated
- before/after screenshot attached
- gate criteria marked Y/N
- regime, location grade, and model fit recorded
- trapped/rewarded participant read recorded
- expected speed compared to actual speed
- mistake taxonomy selected
- entry and exit emotions recorded
- one sentence explaining why trade was taken

## Daily Super-Prompt

After all individual trade autopsies are complete, paste them into this:

```text
Here are my [N] trade autopsies for today: [PASTE ALL]. Total P&L: [X]. Rule adherence count: [X out of N trades followed all rules]. What is the single most important behavioral fix for tomorrow? One sentence only. Not a list.
```
