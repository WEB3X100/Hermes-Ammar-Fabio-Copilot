# 02 - Strategy Library

## Core Mental Model

You are not predicting. You are reading the auction.

The question is not "is this absorption or momentum failure?" The better question is:

```text
Is absorption being used to continue the auction or reverse it?
```

Location defines the question. Order flow defines the answer.

## Alpha/Beta Language

| Term | Meaning | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alpha 1 | Location/context/structure | Tells you where the fight matters |
| Alpha 2 | Absorption at a level | Tells you someone is defending |
| Beta A | Level-based absorption | Aggression hits a defended level and stalls |
| Beta B | Momentum failure over time | Aggression increases but price response weakens |

Memory rule:

```text
Absorption = price refuses to move.
Momentum failure = price stops responding.
Alpha 2 says someone is here.
Beta B says the other side is losing.
```

## The Four Rows

| Metric | Meaning | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total Volume | Effort | How much business is being done? |
| Delta | Directional aggression | Who is pressing? |
| Delta % | One-sidedness | How loud is the aggression? |
| Delta NT | Participation / urgency | Are more traders joining? |
| Total Effort | Total workload | How hard is the auction working? |
| Delta Effort | Result per unit of aggression | Is aggression effective? |
| Price | Outcome | Who won? |

The rule:

```text
Delta % tells you how loud.
Delta Effort tells you how effective.
Delta NT tells you how many are involved.
Price tells you who won.
```

## High-Probability Reversal Profile

Use only at meaningful Alpha 1 locations: supply, demand, POC, VWAP, prior range edge, liquidity draw, VAH/VAL, overnight high/low.

Signature:

- Total Volume high or increasing into the level.
- Delta strongly one-sided.
- Delta % extreme.
- Delta NT one-sided but no longer expanding aggressively.
- Delta Effort low or falling.
- Price does not progress in the direction of aggression.

Interpretation:

```text
Aggression increases. Result decreases. Passive side is absorbing. Aggressive side is exhausting.
```

At demand for longs:

- sellers are loud
- sellers are numerous
- sellers are ineffective
- price stops making meaningful lows

At supply for shorts:

- buyers are loud
- buyers are numerous
- buyers are ineffective
- price stops making meaningful highs

## High-Probability Continuation Profile

Use only when the market has 15+ points of juice to the next zone.

Signature:

- Total Volume stable or slightly declining during pullback.
- Delta one-sided but not accelerating into a wall.
- Delta % stable.
- Delta NT low, controlled, or expanding in the direction of acceptance.
- Delta Effort high.
- Price accepts beyond the level or cleanly retests a flipped zone.

Interpretation:

```text
Aggression is efficient. Price accepts. Do not fade.
```

## Best Differentiator

```text
Reversals show increasing aggression with decreasing results.
Continuations show controlled aggression with stable or accepted results.
```

## Permitted Setup 1 - 9:25-9:35 Immediate Reversal

Purpose: scalp the first clean rejection from a pre-marked zone.

Required:

- price enters a pre-marked supply or demand zone
- 9:30 footprint volume is above 10K
- Alpha 2 absorption appears at location
- Beta B confirms exhaustion or momentum failure
- volume spike occurs at location
- POC retest rejects
- minimum 15 points to target zone
- tight stop below/above the inefficiency

Do not take if:

- 9:30 footprint volume is at or below 10K
- price slices through the zone with efficient acceptance
- Beta B is missing
- you are late by more than 5 ticks from optimal entry

## Permitted Setup 2 - Continuation

Purpose: join initiative movement after weak-hand pullback.

Required:

- 15+ points of clear juice to next zone
- clean second-rotation retest of a freshly flipped zone
- delta confirms continuation, not exhaustion
- Delta Effort stays high
- price accepts in the direction of the move
- no balanced chop profile

Do not take if:

- price is arguing at a hard level
- Delta Effort collapses
- Delta NT stops expanding while price stalls
- no clean retest occurs

## Permitted Setup 3 - 9:45+ Final Reversal

Purpose: wait for better confirmation after the open.

Required:

- multiple rotations complete, ideally at least 3 pushes
- POC retest of liquidation candle
- volume confirmed at location
- Alpha 1 location is valid
- Alpha 2 plus Beta B present
- aggressive side fails to get paid

Do not take if:

- you are entering early before rotations finish
- there is no POC retest
- the setup is just boredom after missing the open

## Permitted Setup 4 - ORB

Purpose: trade opening range continuation only when context aligns.

Required:

- H4 and H1 direction align
- at least 2 VWAP anchors support direction
- 9:30 footprint direction confirms bias
- clear draw on liquidity
- 5-minute footprint volume above 20K
- entry on retest of ORB level, not initial breakout

Do not take if:

- no HTF alignment
- no liquidity draw
- entry is a chase
- 5-minute volume is below threshold

## A+ Setup Promotion

A setup can become A+ only after weekly review shows:

- 5+ occurrences
- win rate above 60%
- average R above 1.5
- no emotional trades included in sample

Until then, it stays a candidate.
