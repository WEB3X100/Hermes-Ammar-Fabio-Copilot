# 08 - Personal Rulebook

These rules exist to protect the trader from the trader.

## Identity

```text
I am a systematic operator, not a gambler.
I follow institutional money flow.
I execute only predetermined ES/NQ setups with defined risk.
Losses are data points. Rule breaks are the enemy.
```

## Phase 1 Scope

- ES and NQ only.
- Coach-only.
- No automated execution.
- Manual DeepCharts screenshots.
- No GC in Phase 1.

## Kill-Switch Rules

- 2 losses / 0 wins = done for the day.
- 1 loss / 1 win = third trade max risk equals current daily profit only.
- 1 huge win = done for the day or manage partials only.
- Daily loss limit triggered = close platform immediately.
- No new trades after 11:30 AM.

## Volume Gates

- Do not trade if 9:30 footprint volume is at or below 10K.
- 5-minute footprint volume must be above 20K before entry.
- No trade unless range to next zone is at least 15 points.

## Pre-Entry Mandates

Before every trade, answer:

```text
What side has momentum?
Am I playing with or against momentum?
If against momentum, what is the momentum-shift justification?
Is this my pattern, or is this my bias forcing action?
```

Required before entry:

- valid location
- A or selected B location grade
- current regime named
- model fits current regime
- named setup type
- screenshot captured
- stop defined
- target defined
- invalidation defined
- trapped/rewarded participant read
- expected speed defined
- hard-rule status safe

## Behavior Rules

- No revenge sizing.
- No adding to a losing position.
- No widening stops.
- No chasing more than 5 ticks from optimal entry.
- No trade because of boredom.
- No trade because "I need to make it back."
- No trade in chop.
- No trade without 15+ points of juice.
- No first-5-minute trade unless immediate reversal criteria are fully met.
- No C-grade or F-grade idea treated as live-ready.
- No trade when the model does not fit the current regime.

## Permitted Setup Universe

Allowed:

- 9:25-9:35 immediate reversal
- Continuation
- 9:45+ final reversal
- ORB

Not allowed:

- random breakout chase
- random supply/demand touch without Alpha/Beta confirmation
- Alpha 2-only reversal without Beta B
- fade of efficient continuation
- post-11:30 revenge trade

## Daily Mantra

```text
Anytime I take a trade, I am putting risk on the table.
It better be worth taking.
```
