## A normal session

| When | Open | Save the completed output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before market | [Pre-market template](playbooks/trading-coach-os/01-pre-market-analysis.md) | Private daily folder: premarket.md |
| During session | [10-second gate and workflow](playbooks/trading-coach-os/03-in-session-workflow.md) | Private notes and screenshots |
| After each trade | [Trade journal](playbooks/trading-coach-os/04-trade-journal-template.md) | Private daily folder: trade-01.md |
| End of day | [Same-day review](playbooks/trading-coach-os/05-same-day-review.md) | Private daily folder: daily-review.md |
| Overnight review | [OS audit](playbooks/trading-coach-os/07-overnight-os-audit.md) | A proposed improvement with evidence |
| End of week | [Weekly audit](playbooks/trading-coach-os/06-weekly-review-audit.md) | Reviewed lesson and next week's focus |
| Before changing a setup | [Validation protocol](playbooks/trading-coach-os/09-backtest-forward-test-protocol.md) | Candidate evidence and review decision |

The private daily folder is `~/TradingCoachLogs/YYYY-MM-DD/`; create it when starting a session. Templates remain here; filled private records remain there.

## Keep the material aligned

The personal rulebook owns live blockers. Research adds context and hypotheses; it does not silently change thresholds. Location grades (A/B/C/F), setup grades (AAA/AA/A/B/No Trade), readiness (WAIT/WATCH/READY/INVALID), and alert labels (Watch/Forming/Human Review/Avoid/Journal) answer different questions. Do not convert one into another automatically. READY means evidence meets a review checklist, not permission for the agent to execute.

The NQ-specific guides do not replace the ES/NQ playbook. Their March 24 case descriptions have no accompanying screenshot or verified year in this import; treat them as historical notes, not current market evidence.

For software work, use [the application guide](../apps/README.md). For missing integrations and planned work, use [status](STATUS.md).
