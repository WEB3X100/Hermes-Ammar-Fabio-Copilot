## Starter Stories

### Absorption Reversal

Aggression increases into a meaningful location, but price stops progressing. Stronger when Delta Effort falls and trapped participants become obvious.

### Efficient Continuation

Price accepts beyond or retests a flipped level while aggression remains effective. Do not fade unless effort/result breaks.

### Balance Edge Fade

Price reaches a defined balance edge and fails to accept outside. Middle-of-range attempts are not part of this story.

### Middle Chop

Location is poor, the story is unclear, or both sides are being rewarded inconsistently. Default output is WAIT or INVALID.
